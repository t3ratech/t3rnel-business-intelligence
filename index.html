Made for how Zimbabwean business actually works

EcoCash-aware capture

Share a payment SMS straight into the app — amounts, references and direction are parsed and proposed to the ledger. Nothing posts without your review below the confidence bar.

Offline-first, honestly

The ledger, capture and books all work with zero signal. The outbox keeps an idempotent queue that drains itself — a retry can never post twice.

USD and ZWG, done properly

Every entry records its rate basis — official, parallel or user-supplied — and provisional rates get confirmed rather than silently changed. Money is integer minor units; there is no float.

ZIMRA fiscal readiness

Invoices carry verification codes and fiscal markings only once the business is validated — onboarding state never prints a fiscal mark it hasn't earned.

Driver profitability

For e-hailing: cost per trip vs fare, time-to-next-ride, and where parking actually pays — computed from your own trip history, on demand, never behind your back.

Your books stay yours

Sign in with Google — no server addresses, no token strings. Exports to CSV and accountant-ready formats whenever you want them.
